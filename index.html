Common questions
What is KINSPACE Ventures?
A co-investment and co-founding model that brings capital, methodology, and market access alongside founders with lived experience in overlooked markets. Each venture is co-founded with the person the product was built for — not pitched to, not advised by, but built with.
What emerging technologies does KINSPACE Ventures work with?
Artificial intelligence, virtual reality, immersive learning, assistive technology, and universal design. Mogo is AI-built assistive technology for children. Bublii applies AI to coaching for difficult workplace conversations. KINSPACE has also worked with Deakin University on VR research, Equal Reality and People Tech Revolution on VR simulations, and Algorand on blockchain product development.
How does KINSPACE co-found with emerging founders?
By bringing the KINSPACE methodology, a decade of global market access, and co-investment alongside founders who have lived experience in a market that has been overlooked. Co-founders are the product authority — not advisors or recipients of funding.
What is the KINSPACE method?
A rights-based approach to intelligence, strategy, and organisational capability. Five questions in every room: who is most impacted, who is not speaking, who is not represented, how am I contributing to the problem, and how can I create space? Applied with Fortune 500 companies, UN agencies, and multinationals across four continents.
What does co-designing with lived experience mean?
The person with lived experience is the co-founder and design authority — not a research subject or focus group participant. Berlin, age 10, is Chief Design Authority of Mogo. Fatima Sampayo co-founded bébé bloom. Jo Rocca co-founded Bublii. Universal design embedded from day one, not retrofitted.
Why does KINSPACE focus on neurodivergent founders?
We are the edge cases. 72% of founders report a mental health condition or neurodivergence. 96% report experiencing discrimination. When we build for the most complex brain in the room, we build better for everyone. We\'re not perfect, but we\'re actively progressing this — and that\'s the point.
